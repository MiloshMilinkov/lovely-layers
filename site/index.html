How ordering works

  1. Send us your cake

    Build it above or pick one of our favourites, then tell us the day you need it. We need two days for most cakes and a week for three tiers.

  2. We call you back

    Within a day, Dunja calls to go over the details, allergies and the final price. Nothing is baked until you say yes.

  3. Pick it up or we bring it

    Collect it from the shop in a sturdy box, or we deliver anywhere in Novi Sad for 500 RSD.

Gluten-free and egg-free sponges are possible for most flavours. Mention it in the notes and we'll talk it through.
